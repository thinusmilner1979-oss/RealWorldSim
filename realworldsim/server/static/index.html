          What is this?

          A probabilistic world model. Every country on earth is simulated day by day: growth, inflation, interest rates, debt, currencies, oil and gas, food, unrest, tension, war.

          It is not a forecast. It is one plausible future drawn from a distribution — change the seed and you get another. The useful question is never "what happens?" but "what happens in most runs?"

          Seeded from real public data (Natural Earth, and World Bank / UCDP / GDELT / FRED via rws sync). Open source, MIT. github.com/thinusmilner1979-oss/RealWorldSim
